# SRHS HMS Study Hub

Year 12 Health and Movement Science revision app for Sarah Redfern High School (NSW HMS 11–12 syllabus, 2023).

- Revision notes, concept breakdowns and summaries for all 36 dot points
- 1-minute narrated revision reels
- Multiple-choice quizzes and a Section I mock
- Short-answer and extended-response practice with marking guidelines
- Revision games

Progress is saved in each student's own browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

## Editing

Content lives in `src/`. Run `./build.sh` (needs Node) to rebuild `index.html`, then commit and push.
